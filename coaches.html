Tacoma Figure Skating Club Coaching Staff

The Tacoma Figure Skating Club has a variety of coaches for you to learn from! Each and every one of them are qualified and ready to help you start or continue your skating journey.

Elaine Jurun

Figure Skating Club Director

Elaine is a passionate figure skater with 30 years of experience competing and performing and 15 years of experience with coaching! As a singles and pairs skater and coach, she has competed nationally and international levels, earning numerous medals in technical and showcase events.

As Figure Skating Club Director, Elaine oversees majority of the day-to-day operations for the TFSC and the rink's Learn-To-Skate program. She also holds a Bachelor of Fine Art from Cornish College of the Art in Seattle, bringing her artistic background and creative perspective into her figure skating.

Contact Elaine

Jessica Jones

Basics/Dance Coach

Jessica is an experienced figure skater who grew up in Canada skating alongside today's Olympians! She has been skating for 25 years and brings great energy on and off the rink. She was formerly an ice dance competitor competing nationally and internationally.

As the basics coach, she ensures that all skaters have a sturdy foundation for the rest of their skating skills. Fundamentals are essential to everything and there is no better edge teacher than a dance coach. Alongside basics, she helps skaters improve their artistry and dancing on ice.

Contact Jessica

Paulina Paloma

Freestyle Coach

Paulina is a skater who started 10 years ago as an adult skater, ever since then she has dedicated herself to learning the freestyle techniques in depth and has also won medals multiple years in a row at adult nationals.

As a freestyle coach, Paulina is well versed in rotational form, muscle control, jumping form, and spinning mechanics. She also knows ISI and ISU scoring for testing and competitions and will help optimize your programs for your skating.

Contact Paulina
