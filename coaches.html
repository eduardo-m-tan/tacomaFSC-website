Tacoma Figure Skating Club Coaching Staff

The Tacoma Figure Skating Club has a variety of coaches for you to learn from! Each and every one of them are qualified and ready to help you start or continue your skating journey.

Elaine Jurun

Figure Skating Club Director

Elaine is a passionate figure skater with 30 years of experience competing and performing and 15 years of experience with coaching! As a singles and pairs skater and coach, she has competed nationally and international levels, earning numerous medals in technical and showcase events.

As Figure Skating Club Director, Elaine oversees majority of the day-to-day operations for the TFSC and the rink's Learn-To-Skate program. She also holds a Bachelor of Fine Art from Cornish College of the Art in Seattle, bringing her artistic background and creative perspective into her figure skating.
