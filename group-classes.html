Learn-To-Skate

The Tacoma Skating School is the perfect place to start by learning foundations! Every discipline of skating begins with fun- and goal-oriented beginner classes. You won't just be learning how to skate though, you will learn how to skate fast as the wind and how to use the skates as an extension of you.

Learn-To-Skate is supported by the Olympic/National governing bodies of the U.S. Speed Skating, U.S. Hockey, and U.S. Figure Skating Association to spread the health, social, and developmental benefits that can be gained through ice skating! Where will skating take you?

Let's Get Started

Classes are rolling every 6 weeks, available on Saturdays, Mondays, and Wednesdays and you must have a Learn-To-Skate USA Membership to enroll in our program.

L-T-S is a one-hour session split into 30-minutes of class and 30-minutes of individual practice where the skater can practice whatever they may like.

There are two packages available, one comes with rental skates while the other does not, we also offer a family pass if you are a parent wanting to join your skater for their practice sessions after class.
