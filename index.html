About Us

Tacoma Figure Skating Club is a non-profit organization and a welcoming community of skaters in the South Puget Sound area, our members range from Basic Skills to Adult Skaters to National Qualifying Series Skaters! We want to help you take your skating to the next level with:

  • Daily freestyle sessions at Tacoma Twin Rinks
  • Qualified coaching staff
  • Off- and on-ice training opportunities
  • Virtual test sessions each month
  • In-house showcases
  • But most importantly, we foster a supportive and inclusive environment for all skaters!

Visiting Information

When visiting Tacoma Twin Rinks, there are a few things to keep in mind!

  • Registration must be purchased online, through a punch card, or cash at the front desk, and all skaters need to check-in at the front desk.
  • Skate rentals are available within the L-T-S packages and they're included in the public session admission!
  • For all skaters, we have locker rooms available to get your skates on and to get yourself warmed up, each one with a heater that may be turned on dependent on the season and/or weather.
  • Spectators may choose to sit on the benches next to the rental window or upstairs where there is more seating available.
  • Additionally, there is a training area upstairs that skaters may use with the approval of their TFSC coach and management staff.
  • Tacoma Twin Rinks has plenty of parking available, including accessible parking to those who may need it.
