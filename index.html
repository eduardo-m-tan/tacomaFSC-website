Get Started Here!

Welcome to the Tacoma Figure Skating Club website! If you are looking to get started in figure skating, or if you are a seasoned skater looking for a supportive community, you've come to the right place! We have Learn-to-Skate classes throughout the whole year, and of many different levels for skaters of any age! If you are a skater looking to continue your figure skating journey, learn more about us and our offerings below, and check out our schedule to see when you can come skate with us!

About Us

Tacoma Figure Skating Club is a non-profit organization and a welcoming community of skaters in the South Puget Sound area, our members range from Basic Skills to Adult Skaters to National Qualifying Series Skaters! We want to help you take your skating to the next level with:

  • Daily freestyle sessions at Tacoma Twin Rinks
  • Qualified coaching staff
  • Off- and on-ice training opportunities
  • Virtual test sessions each month
  • In-house showcases
  • But most importantly, we foster a supportive and inclusive environment for all skaters!

Upcoming Events

Tacoma Figure Skating Club hosts a variety of events throughout the year, we have showcases, skating camps, and we also host an annual award banquet for all Tacoma Figure Skating Club members! Take a look at our upcoming events!

Costume Showcase Party on Ice

When: October 30th at 6:15 P.M.

Join us for our annual Costume Showcase Party on Ice! Get ready for thrills, chills, and a whole lot of fun! Maybe it's spooky, maybe it's mysterious, maybe it's just plain silly! Whatever the program may be, we hope to see you there! This event is open to all local skaters and it is a great opportunity for you or your skater to showcase their skills!

Holiday Tradition on Ice

When: December 20th at 6:15 P.M.

Celebrate the holiday season with us as we showcase our skaters' talents in our Holiday Tradition on Ice showcase! Come in your best holiday attire and enjoy a night of skating, music, and holiday cheer! This event is open to all local skaters and it is a great opportunity for you or your skater to showcase their skills!
