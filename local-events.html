38th Annual Ice Crystal Classic

October 23rd-25th, 2026

Mountain View Ice Arena, Vancouver, WA

This is a competition open to most levels, see website for details, registration closed September 6th, 2026.

The Ice Crystal Classic is an annual competition that the Portland Ice Skating Club has been hosting for now 38 years! We welcome skaters of many levels in Compete U.S.A. as well as adults, singles, solo dance, showcase, excel and adaptive skating!

2026 Saatva Skate America

November 13th-15th, 2026

Angel of The Winds Arena, Everett, WA

This is an advanced level event where competing is invite only. Tickets may be purchased for viewing.

Skate America returns to Everett, Washington, November 13-15, 2026, bringing one of figure skating's premier international events back to the Pacific Northwest for the first time since 2018. As part of the ISU Grand Prix of Figure Skating Series, the competition will showcase many of the world's top athletes as they begin their quest for a spot in the Grand Prix Final.

Fans will experience three days of world-class competition across all four disciplines, including men's and women's singles, pairs, and ice dance. The star-studded lineup features reigning U.S. Champion Amber Glenn, World Champion and Olympic gold medalist Ilia Malinin, and two-time Olympian Jason Brown. Also taking the ice are top U.S. pairs team Alisa Efimova and Misha Mitrofanov, along with rising ice dance stars Emilea Zingas and Vadym Kolesnik. (lineup subject to change).
