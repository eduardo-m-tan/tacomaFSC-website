Local Events near or around Washington!

There are many events outside of the rink that skaters may be interested in like showcases, competitions, testing sessions, or skating camps. Depending on what you or your skater are looking for, there should be something for you!

38th Annual Ice Crystal Classic

October 23rd-25th, 2026

Mountain View Ice Arena, Vancouver, WA

This is a competition open to most levels, see website for details, registration closed September 6th, 2026.

The Ice Crystal Classic is an annual competition that the Portland Ice Skating Club has been hosting for now 38 years! We welcome skaters of many levels in Compete U.S.A. as well as adults, singles, solo dance, showcase, excel and adaptive skating!

Learn More!

Bremerton FSC's Cheers For Fears

October 24th, 2026

Bremerton Ice Center

This showcase is open to all skaters of any level.

Bremerton Figure Skating Club will be hosting Cheers for Fears again this year! Registration is open until October 1st, 2026. Skaters of all levels will be showcasing their spooky programs and there will be a trunk or treat available as well!

Learn More!

2026 Saatva Skate America

November 13th-15th, 2026

Angel of The Winds Arena, Everett, WA

This is an advanced level event where competing is invite only. Tickets may be purchased for viewing.

Skate America is an ISU Grand Prix Series event where top skaters from across the globe will be competing for their spot in the Grand Prix Final. Fans have the chance to experience three days of world-class competition and skating across all four disciplines. Skaters like Amber Glenn and Jason Brown will be along the lineup.
